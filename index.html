Questions owners ask

Is Vasool a real product?

Vasool is a design case study. The demo uses sample data and the whole page runs on HTML and CSS alone. Nothing you type is saved.

Will reminders feel rude to my customers?

Every reminder can start Gentle. Clear and Firm are one tap away, and Vasool suggests the tone that fits how late the payment is.

What if a customer promises a date and does not pay?

Vasool records the promise, flags it when the date passes, and suggests a kind next step based on that customer’s history. When the money arrives, the promise closes by itself. See the full Promise to Pay workflow.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. A reminder is a plain message your customer can read on any phone.

Can I bring in my existing notebook?

You add your customers and open invoices once. After that, Vasool keeps the running total for each customer.

Does Vasool replace my accounting software?

No. It does one job: collecting what you are owed. It sits beside your billing or accounts tool.
