Step

1. The reminder goes out

What Vasool does

The distributor sends a Clear reminder. The customer sees quick replies, so answering takes one tap.

Design choice

Make it easy to say “when”, not just “ok”. Quick replies turn silence into a date.

Step

2. The customer replies in their own words

What Vasool does

Vasool reads “Friday ko payment kar dunga” and proposes a promise: ₹48,000 by Fri 20 Nov.

Design choice

Never record silently. The distributor confirms with one tap, so a wrong guess never reaches the books.

Step

3. The promise is recorded

What Vasool does

Vasool confirms the date back to the customer and pauses reminders until the promised day.

Design choice

Stop chasing someone who has just given you a date. It protects the relationship.

Step

4. Friday passes with no payment

What Vasool does

Vasool flags the customer and suggests the next step, based on this customer’s history.

Design choice

First miss means a gentle check-in. Repeated misses move toward a call, then Firm. Never accuse.

Step

5. Payment arrives, promise closes

What Vasool does

Vasool matches the payment to the invoices, closes the promise and sends the customer a receipt.

Design choice

Nothing for the owner to tidy up. The promise history stays, so next time the suggestion is smarter.

Back to dashboard

Ask for a date, not a reason

A date can be tracked and respected. A reason invites an argument.

Escalate by history, not by anger

The next action depends on how this customer has behaved before, so a good customer is never treated like a bad one.

Close the loop automatically

Matching payments to promises removes bookkeeping, so owners trust the list.
